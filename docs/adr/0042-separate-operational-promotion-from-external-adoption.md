# Separate Operational Promotion from External Adoption

Operational Promotion may autonomously update Reflex's internal Knowledge and Model Revisions, but Reflex does not silently replace code or assets in an external project. It exports verified Artifacts with Measurements, provenance, and Verification Records, and the caller separately authorizes External Adoption.

## Considered Options

Automatically applying every internally preferred Artifact would maximize end-to-end autonomy but extend Reflex's authority beyond optimization into mutation of consumer systems, where integration constraints and deployment consequences are not captured by Verification alone.
