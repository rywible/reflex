# Measure contextual Potential with Shadow Campaigns

Status: accepted; supersedes ADR-0029

Reflex may buy a bounded Shadow Campaign as an information Investment by forking two resource-matched arms from one restart-complete state, permitting one Artifact, Derived Operator, Model component, Knowledge Revision, or Runtime Policy Revision in treatment and masking only that subject in control. The paired contrast records contextual downstream consequences such as verified discoveries, descendants, reuse, compression, and resources saved; observational ranks remain diagnostics and neither form of evidence establishes correctness or Scientific Confirmation.

## Consequences

Both arms pin identical semantic revisions, goals, scheduling semantics, indexed random streams, and resource sub-envelopes. The Open transition reserves the complete paired request allowance before dispatch. Each arm then charges the complete live fork graph—including restart bytes, captured proposals, fate-vector capacities, both plans, replayed Artifact storage, deferred recovery candidates, and pipeline staging—before growth, subtracts it from the Kernel allowance, and attributes it alongside external-worker CPU, elapsed time, and peak resident memory. An asymmetric, interrupted, resource-mismatched, or otherwise incomparable pair is invalidated rather than converted into a causal Training Target, and immutable Protected Allocations cap total shadow spending.

Potential training consumes only terminal, symmetric `ContextualContrast` records bound to the exact challenged subject; it never recomputes a label from current state. Verified downstream descendants, cross-goal leverage, compression, mechanically enabled Operators, and CPU saved populate separate forecast axes. Correctness remains absent from these causal Potential labels because only the Domain Verification Kernel can establish it.
