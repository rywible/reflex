# Coordinate Goals through bounded Campaigns

Reflex organizes work as a Campaign: a bounded portfolio of Optimization Goals within one domain, sharing a Discovery Graph and total search budget. A single-goal run remains the degenerate case, while concurrent Goals allow reusable Artifacts, delayed credit, and Emergent Opportunities to create cross-goal value without granting an open-ended research process unlimited compute.

## Considered Options

Independent Optimization Runs would simplify ownership and accounting but hide reuse, compression, and cross-goal Potential. One unbounded global research economy would maximize sharing but make resource control and the meaning of value too diffuse.

## Consequences

Intent lives in an Agenda Graph separate from the verified knowledge in the Discovery Graph. Campaign allocation must account for both direct progress and expected downstream value, all Goals in a Campaign share domain semantics, and every Campaign has an explicit finite budget.
