# Protect exploration across correctness claims

Each Runtime selection epoch on a multi-claim Search Frontier reserves one Candidate per Seed-relative correctness claim before learned ranking, uncertainty sampling, deterministic fallback ranking, or remaining Derived Operator exploration spends the Verification allowance, but only when the allowance can cover the complete represented set. Protected Derived Operator Candidates are preferred for this reservation because they combine claim coverage with discovery potential. Single-claim frontiers pay no diversity tax. When the allowance cannot cover the complete set, active Preference and ordinary ranking retain authority rather than an arbitrary partial reservation overriding caller economics.

Global Candidate ordering previously allowed structurally small or early Seeds to consume an entire finite envelope: a 16-Seed Lean training run recorded 1,008 attempts but covered only 13 claims, making a disjoint eight-Replay/eight-Selection promotion decision impossible. Per-claim protected exploration guarantees economically diverse evidence without scalarizing Goals or trusting a model. It can spend checks on unpromising claims, but that bounded cost is necessary for autonomous learning, and every proposal remains subject to the Verification Kernel. The scheduling change advances the Runtime revision so interrupted Sessions fail rather than resume under different allocation semantics.
