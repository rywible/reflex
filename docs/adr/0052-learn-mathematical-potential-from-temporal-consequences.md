# Learn mathematical Potential from temporal consequences

Lean mathematical taste is learned from replayable consequences across pre-cutoff Temporal Snapshot Pairs: later anticipation, verified descendants, reuse, proof and family collapse, corpus compression, verification cost, and dead ends. Reflex retains these as separate Training Targets and an Elegance Profile rather than treating theorem popularity, names, human prose, or one scalar reward as ground truth. This lets a small CPU model learn long-horizon judgment while the Runtime Controller remains free to choose among incomparable outcomes under the active Optimization Goals.

## Consequences

Every cross-snapshot relationship must retain enough identity and kernel evidence to replay under the later environment. Semantic Migration replays Artifacts and rebuilds features, retrieval indexes, relationships, calibration, and model state; it never copies learned bytes across Semantic Identities or asks a human to repair failures. The post-cutoff Temporal Audit Corpus remains unavailable until the implementation, treatments, analysis, and immutable protocol are frozen.
