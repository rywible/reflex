# Separate training, operational, and scientific data

Continuous improvement assigns Experience Ledger material to distinct roles: diversity-preserving Replay Corpora produce Training Targets, operational Selection Corpora compare revisions for promotion, and sealed Scientific Corpora never influence search, training, tuning, or Operational Promotion. Replay retains failures and older experience so recent self-generated successes cannot erase negative knowledge or create an unchecked feedback loop.

## Considered Options

Training and promoting against the same accumulated history would maximize data use but reward overfitting and make regressions difficult to detect. Feeding sealed scientific cases back into improvement would increase measured performance while destroying their ability to confirm claims independently.
