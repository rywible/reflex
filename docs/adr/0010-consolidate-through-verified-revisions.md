# Consolidate through verified Knowledge Revisions

Reflex periodically proposes a challenger Knowledge Revision that deduplicates structure, factors shared derivations, extracts reusable knowledge, and may introduce generalized Artifacts when their claims are independently Verified and their value is grounded in improving existing knowledge. Consolidation preserves original evidence and provenance, then earns atomic promotion by improving representative held-out Campaigns across domain Measurements rather than merely minimizing graph size.

## Considered Options

Pure storage compaction would be safer but would miss the proof-collapsing abstractions and reusable Operators that make external knowledge more capable. Unchecked generalization would produce stronger compression but violate Reflex's verified-knowledge invariant and allow consolidation to become hidden synthesis.

## Consequences

Active Campaigns pin an immutable Knowledge Revision for reproducibility. Consolidation operates as a challenger, verifies every semantic addition, measures compression alongside downstream search productivity, and cannot discard the durable evidence needed to reconstruct or audit promoted knowledge.
