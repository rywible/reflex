# Separate observations from Training Targets

The Experience Ledger preserves immutable observations, while versioned derivation logic produces revisable Training Targets. Credit may be reassigned when later evidence shows that an Artifact enabled descendants, served other Optimization Goals, or improved Knowledge Consolidation; Model Revisions record the exact target derivation they used.

## Considered Options

Freezing labels when an event occurs would simplify online training but erase the delayed consequences that define Potential. Mutating the original observations would make updated credit convenient but destroy provenance and prevent faithful retraining.
