# Activate Derived Operators immediately

Status: superseded by ADR-0077

A Derived Operator produced by verified Knowledge Consolidation becomes eligible for search immediately, without first passing a separate operational usefulness gate. Its Candidates still pass the Verification Kernel, so activation cannot weaken correctness even when the operator later proves economically unhelpful.

## Considered Options

Pre-activation evaluation could prevent search-vocabulary bloat, but would delay reuse and introduce a second admission barrier for knowledge that the autonomous allocator can evaluate through ordinary experience.
