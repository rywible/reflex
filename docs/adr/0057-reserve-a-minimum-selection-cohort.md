# Reserve a minimum Selection cohort

The Learning Module assigns new correctness-claim groups deterministically within each training niche from their persistent first-seen ordinal in the complete Experience Ledger. Ordinals zero through seven are Replay, eight through fifteen are disjoint Selection, and later distinct claims assign ordinal sixteen and every fifth ordinal thereafter to Selection. Bounded training scans may slide over Experience, but they never renumber a retained claim or change its role. A challenger is eligible only when its own niche retains at least eight Replay and eight Selection claims. An assigned Selection case remains withheld even before the opening eight-case Selection cohort is complete. Selection cases rotate into Replay only after bounded operational use, so only fresh operational evidence can replenish the promotion gate.

A fixed hash lottery preserved disjointness but made the minimum eight-case promotion rule operationally unpredictable: small domains commonly needed around forty claims before any Model Revision could be promoted, and a causal experiment could silently compare two Bootstrap revisions. A global assignment could also let one well-populated Operator family satisfy the gate for an unrelated Specialist. The exact per-niche schedule permits meaningful learning from sixteen diverse claims without training on the promotion evidence or borrowing corpus coverage across routes. The changed assignment schedule advances the Runtime revision so interrupted Sessions cannot silently resume under different learning semantics.
