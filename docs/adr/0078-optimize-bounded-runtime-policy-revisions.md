# Optimize bounded Runtime Policy Revisions

Reflex may optimize its own search economics through immutable Runtime Policy Revisions expressed as bounded canonical data and interpreted beneath an immutable Runtime Controller. A built-in policy kernel establishes totality, deterministic bounded execution, Resource Envelope conservation, protected minima, corpus isolation, and inability to alter Verification or Admission authority; passing challengers must then earn Operational Promotion through controlled comparison.

## Considered Options

Keeping scheduling constants permanently hand-authored would prevent the system from learning how to spend compute. Automatically compiling or replacing arbitrary Rust would be more expressive but would make the candidate system part of its own authority and make safe rollback, bounded evaluation, and portable recovery substantially harder.

## Consequences

Policy candidates may eventually change cohort sizing, routing, exploration above immutable floors, shortlist widths, and training, consolidation, shadow, stopping, or reinvestment cadence. A field is eligible for autonomous challenge only after the paired policy trial actually executes that field throughout both restart-complete arms. The current policy neighborhood therefore changes only Verification-cohort size, which the bounded multi-cohort arm scheduler directly exercises while immutable Bootstrap and Verification floors remain in force. Unevaluated fields remain fixed rather than receiving receipt-only promotion evidence. Policy data cannot contain executable Rust or invent Optimization Goals. Reflex may export separately Verified source or binary improvements, but installing them remains External Adoption.

The Runtime Policy component authenticated by `IntelligenceCore` is the sole current policy authority. A terminal Shadow Campaign outcome and its `PolicyUpdate` are staged in one Core transition, and the resulting checkpoint is durably published before the next cohort observes a changed policy. Current restart bundles carry no standalone policy payload. Completed legacy bundles may import that payload exactly once into an eligible migrated Core; interrupted legacy bundles remain incompatible.
