# Keep Goals externally grounded

Optimization Goals must be supplied by the caller; a Domain Definition may offer templates but cannot start improvement by itself. Reflex constructs Campaigns and autonomously explores Opportunities but does not invent Goals to justify its own work. Locally unimpressive stepping-stone Artifacts may receive further investment through Potential, while Operators and Verifiers may mechanically induce formal Obligations that must be discharged for correctness.

## Considered Options

Autonomously generated Instrumental Goals could make long-horizon planning explicit, but they would blur optimization with synthesis and permit Reflex to manufacture value by creating Goals it can satisfy. Attaching Potential to Artifacts and Opportunities supports long-horizon exploration while keeping value traceable to externally grounded Goals.

## Consequences

The Campaign planner controls what work to schedule but not what ultimately counts as valuable. Artifact retention and search allocation must permit dominated stepping stones, and any future system that invents new terminal mathematical Goals is a distinct synthesis capability rather than hidden optimizer behavior.
