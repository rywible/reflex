# Separate result quality, retention, and search investment

Verification, Admission to the Discovery Graph, membership in a Goal's Pareto Frontier, and membership in the Search Frontier are independent decisions. Reflex explicitly admits selected Verified Candidates rather than retaining all valid output, while the Search Frontier may invest in dominated stepping stones whose novelty, information value, or Potential could lead to valuable descendants.

## Consequences

The Experience Ledger records every attempt even when its Candidate is not admitted. Admission protects resident memory from repetitive valid junk, and search allocation cannot be implemented as hill-climbing over only the current Pareto Frontier.
