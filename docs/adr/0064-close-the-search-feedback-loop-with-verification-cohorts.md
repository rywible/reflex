# Close the search feedback loop with Verification Cohorts

The Runtime spends Candidate Verification through bounded Verification Cohorts rather than sending the complete remaining allowance through one batch. One cohort is one selection transaction, one kernel batch, and one Admission feedback step. After its outcomes become restart-complete, allocation is reconsidered under the updated Experience Ledger and Search Frontier before another cohort is selected.

Cohort size is private Runtime policy, not Domain configuration or a caller tuning knob. It is capped by the remaining Verification allowance and is at least the larger of eight Candidates and the Verification Kernel's worker lanes. When the remaining allowance can cover every represented correctness claim that has no retained Verification Experience, the cohort also covers those claims. For more than eight uncovered claims it reserves eight additional ordinary slots, preventing the coverage obligation itself from starving learned and Bootstrap allocation. Once a claim has any retained verified attempt, it no longer consumes a protected-origin slot merely because another cohort began. This supersedes ADR 0058's requirement to repeat claim protection in every selection epoch; its complete-or-none coverage rule remains in force for uncovered claims.

Candidates not selected into the current cohort remain deferred in Runtime-owned memory. They receive an immutable Policy-Deferred Candidate Fate when first generated, then a new Fate when later selected; merely carrying the same Candidate through another cohort does not publish a quadratic series of duplicate deferrals. Fresh policy ranks and epoch-sensitive features are recomputed before selection. Newly generated Candidates are deduplicated against deferred work with the deferred copy first, so a finite generation window cannot erase work that was already considered. The Search Frontier retains prior parents and appends newly admitted Artifacts, keeping deferred source indexes valid and allowing both old opportunities and verified descendants to compete after feedback.

Deferred work is exact recovery state, not an inference from a shrinking generation prefix. Every interrupted Domain Bundle canonically encodes Search Frontier membership and order, each deferred Candidate with its parent key, Operator, proposal metadata, and domain-owned Artifact representation, and the round-robin queue of admitted parents with their per-Operator Primitive Enumeration Cursors. Recovery resolves that state against the exact reconstructed Search Frontier and installed Operator catalog before generating another page. A completed Session that exhausted its Resource Envelope also retains this unfinished search tail for a later Session with a fresh envelope; deliberate observer stops and satisfied Success Conditions do not. Candidate Fates, verified attempts, Admission, deferred work, and pending generation state cross one durability barrier before another cohort begins.

A cohort is an atomic transaction. The Runtime conservatively checks resident and durable capacity before kernel dispatch and charges the exact frontier keys, pending-parent cursors, prospective deferred Candidate storage, selected Candidate pipeline, epoch context, coexisting checkpoint buffers, and durability bytes according to their post-selection ownership. Moved Artifact payloads are charged once as required by ADR 0072. After a verdict exists, deadlines are observed only after Measurement, Admission, and the cohort checkpoint complete. A post-kernel resource refusal aborts the Session with the prior restart-complete checkpoint intact instead of publishing Accepted Experience without its Artifact or silently completing a partial cohort.

## Trade-offs

Smaller cohorts add selection, checkpoint, and external-kernel batch overhead. A minimum of eight and the worker-lane floor preserve useful CPU parallelism, while claim-aware expansion avoids turning a large Seed Scope into hundreds of single-item batches. Cohort checkpoints are synchronously durable before reconsideration so process CPU attribution cannot overlap a prior durability write. The policy deliberately prefers verified feedback latency over maximum one-shot throughput because the latter prevented any discovery from influencing its own Optimization Run.

Keeping earlier parents on the Search Frontier increases index work. Generation gives each selected pending parent bounded Primitive pages and one bounded Derived Operator sample; small represented claim sets receive complete parent coverage, while larger sets retain exact progress and rotate incomplete parents behind unvisited work. The Runtime removes a parent only after every Primitive Operator reports a final page and Derived exploration has run, and advances progress only in the same transaction that retains generated Candidates or verified outcomes. ADR 0070 supplies the private resumable cursor that this record originally deferred.

Generation maintains an inventory of eight alternatives per next-cohort Verification plus enough breadth to visit every primitive Operator across up to two cohorts of pending parents, subject to the remaining Verification allowance and resident capacity. Already deferred Candidates count toward that target, so later epochs generate only the refill deficit defined by ADR 0071. One cohort's parent breadth alone can erase the deferred counterfactual pool when protected exploration occupies the complete first cohort; unbounded alternative depth instead materializes heavyweight Candidates that cannot fit the Resource Envelope before selection. The combined bound preserves a subsequent learned-versus-Bootstrap allocation opportunity while keeping generation proportional to the feedback unit rather than the whole Session allowance.

Verification Cohorts change allocation, Search Frontier, Candidate Fate, and recovery semantics. Runtime revision 10 rejects bundles from before cohorting, revision 13 pins the bounded cohort-alternative and two-cohort operator-breadth lookahead, revision 15 pins restart-complete Primitive enumeration pages, revision 16 pins deferred-inventory backpressure, and revision 17 pins move-aware Verification admission. Internal Lean Development schemas advance. The public Improvement Session configuration remains unchanged.
