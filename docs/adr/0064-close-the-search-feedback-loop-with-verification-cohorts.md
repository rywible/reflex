# Close the search feedback loop with Verification Cohorts

The Runtime spends Candidate Verification through bounded Verification Cohorts rather than sending the complete remaining allowance through one batch. One cohort is one selection transaction, one kernel batch, and one Admission feedback step. After its outcomes become restart-complete, allocation is reconsidered under the updated Experience Ledger and Search Frontier before another cohort is selected.

Cohort size is private Runtime policy, not Domain configuration or a caller tuning knob. It is capped by the remaining Verification allowance and is at least the larger of eight Candidates and the Verification Kernel's worker lanes. When the remaining allowance can cover every represented correctness claim that has no retained Verification Experience, the cohort also covers those claims. For more than eight uncovered claims it reserves eight additional ordinary slots, preventing the coverage obligation itself from starving learned and Bootstrap allocation. Once a claim has any retained verified attempt, it no longer consumes a protected-origin slot merely because another cohort began. This supersedes ADR 0058's requirement to repeat claim protection in every selection epoch; its complete-or-none coverage rule remains in force for uncovered claims.

Candidates not selected into the current cohort remain deferred in Runtime-owned memory. They receive an immutable Policy-Deferred Candidate Fate when first generated, then a new Fate when later selected; merely carrying the same Candidate through another cohort does not publish a quadratic series of duplicate deferrals. Fresh policy ranks and epoch-sensitive features are recomputed before selection. Newly generated Candidates are deduplicated against deferred work with the deferred copy first, so a finite generation window cannot erase work that was already considered. The Search Frontier retains prior parents and appends newly admitted Artifacts, keeping deferred source indexes valid and allowing both old opportunities and verified descendants to compete after feedback.

Deferred work is exact recovery state, not an inference from a shrinking generation prefix. Every interrupted Domain Bundle canonically encodes Search Frontier membership and order, each deferred Candidate with its parent key, Operator, proposal metadata, and domain-owned Artifact representation, and the keys of admitted parents that have not yet passed through generation. Recovery resolves those keys against the exact reconstructed Search Frontier, rejects missing parents or Operators, and resumes the exact queue before generating from pending parents. A completed Session that exhausted its Resource Envelope also retains this unfinished search tail for a later Session with a fresh envelope; deliberate observer stops and satisfied Success Conditions do not. Candidate Fates, verified attempts, Admission, deferred work, and pending generation state cross one durability barrier before another cohort begins.

A cohort is an atomic transaction. The Runtime conservatively checks resident and durable capacity before kernel dispatch and charges the exact frontier keys, pending-parent keys, deferred Candidate storage, and encoded checkpoint as distinct live allocations. After a verdict exists, deadlines are observed only after Measurement, Admission, and the cohort checkpoint complete. A post-kernel resource refusal aborts the Session with the prior restart-complete checkpoint intact instead of publishing Accepted Experience without its Artifact or silently completing a partial cohort.

## Trade-offs

Smaller cohorts add selection, checkpoint, and external-kernel batch overhead. A minimum of eight and the worker-lane floor preserve useful CPU parallelism, while claim-aware expansion avoids turning a large Seed Scope into hundreds of single-item batches. Cohort checkpoints are synchronously durable before reconsideration so process CPU attribution cannot overlap a prior durability write. The policy deliberately prefers verified feedback latency over maximum one-shot throughput because the latter prevented any discovery from influencing its own Optimization Run.

Keeping earlier parents on the Search Frontier increases index work. Generation gives each selected pending parent its own bounded primitive and Derived Operator windows; small represented claim sets receive complete parent coverage, while larger sets retain an exact unprocessed tail instead of spreading every window too thin. The Runtime removes only materialized parent keys, and only in the same durable transaction that retains their generated Candidates or verified outcomes. Dropping parents would make cohorting lose deferred opportunities or require a public resumable-Operator cursor contract. The retained frontier is the smaller interface and remains bounded by admitted Artifacts and the Resource Envelope.

Generation materializes at most two Verification Cohorts of lookahead, with eight Candidate choices per prospective Verification slot, subject to the remaining Verification allowance and resident capacity. One cohort alone can erase the deferred counterfactual pool when protected exploration occupies the complete first cohort; unbounded lookahead instead materializes heavyweight Candidates that cannot fit the Resource Envelope before selection. Two cohorts preserve a subsequent learned-versus-Bootstrap allocation opportunity while keeping generation proportional to the feedback unit rather than the whole Session allowance.

Verification Cohorts change allocation, Search Frontier, Candidate Fate, and recovery semantics. Runtime revision 10 rejects bundles from before cohorting, and revision 12 pins the bounded two-cohort generation lookahead rather than silently resuming under the former whole-envelope window. Internal Lean Development schemas advance. The public Improvement Session and Domain Definition interfaces remain unchanged.
