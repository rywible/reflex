# Let evidence govern Derived Operator allocation

Immediate activation makes a new Derived Operator eligible and grants enough initial exploration to observe its behavior, not a permanent claim on resources. Ordinary experience then informs allocation, and later Knowledge Revisions may prioritize, specialize, or deactivate the operator while retaining its provenance and history.

## Considered Options

Permanent resource entitlement would allow accumulated operators to consume the search budget indefinitely. Requiring a usefulness gate before any activation would prevent the system from collecting the evidence needed to learn when a novel operator matters.
