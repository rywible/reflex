# Lean 2026 Public Optimizer Temporal Audit v2

Status: **registered design; implementation and audit declarations still sealed**

This protocol replaces v1 before audit exposure. It tests the production Optimizer exclusively through `improve`: the Experimental Harness may prepare pinned Domain Definitions, Optimization Goals, Resource Envelopes, Seed Scopes, and Domain Bundle treatments, but it may not train, rank, admit, consolidate, or inspect the Search Frontier itself.

## Claim

A small CPU-only Reflex Improvement Session trained on verified Lean proof optimization through 2024 allocates a constrained held-out session better than registered counterfactuals and produces Verified Artifacts with greater independently measured mathematical Potential before July 2026. Scientific Confirmation requires both the pre-cutoff public-path causal gate and the sealed temporal gate; neither a standalone predictor nor a favorable theorem is sufficient.

## Pre-cutoff production sessions

All sessions use the pinned 2024-12-31 Lean Semantic Identity so a Domain Bundle is resumed only under identical semantics. The training Seed Scope contains declarations already present by September 2024. The held-out Seed Scope contains December declarations whose semantic families occur in neither June nor September. Exact declaration, source-module, family, goal, treatment, and corpus identities are frozen before future exposure.

The training session receives four separate proof Optimization Goals: proof nodes, proof depth, encoded proof bytes, and allowed axioms. Full resumes the production-created training Domain Bundle on the held-out Seed Scope. Bootstrap starts from a fresh Bootstrap Revision. No-model resumes the same verified knowledge and Experience with the learned Model Revision replaced by the production-created Bootstrap Revision. No-consolidation resumes the same Experience and Model Revision with Derived Operators removed and its Knowledge Revision recomputed. Every treatment enters the held-out session through `improve` with an equal Resource Envelope.

The harness freezes the complete ordered stream of held-out Pareto additions and the final held-out Pareto Artifacts produced by each treatment. It does not manufacture per-head rankings. Training, restore, search, Verification, Admission, learning, and Knowledge Consolidation usage come from `SessionOutcome::usage` plus the resource-accounted Lean worker reports.

Every resource-intensive development and confirmatory child runs inside an authenticated host-safety envelope outside its equal public Resource Envelope. The registered Linux execution reserves at least one logical CPU and 16 GiB of host memory from the complete child process tree and memory-backed work files. The child must observe and record the kernel-enforced memory boundary and CPU affinity before loading either catalog; absent isolation is a refusal, and reaching the outer boundary is a retained failure rather than permission to consume the reserve.

## Pre-cutoff causal gate

Before any 2026 checkout, Full must beat Bootstrap and both one-factor ablations on verified held-out Pareto progress under the preregistered multiobjective comparison, with zero correctness, protected-Measurement, recovery, or Resource Envelope regression. Cold replay must reproduce every retained Artifact and Verification Record. A failure here is a retained Null Result and forbids audit exposure; Wrela still follows as the registered third domain.

## Sealed temporal gate

Only after the causal result, implementation, manifest, and exact audit lock are committed may the latest mathlib commit strictly before 2026-07-01 be materialized. The official Lean kernel replays every frozen Artifact without repair. Future direct descendants, theorem reuse, exact-statement compression, declaration survival, dependency cost, dead-end risk, and certified exact/definitional/specialization/derivation/collapse/compression relationships remain separate Measurements.

The statistical unit is a semantic theorem family nested in its exact source module. Comparisons are paired by the frozen family identity, retain missing and failed Artifacts as zero utility, and use a deterministic two-stage module/family bootstrap with simultaneous one-sided 99% bounds. The exact practical thresholds and 1/4/16/64 CPU-hour anytime decision rule are content-addressed in the executable manifest before exposure.

The mechanical report is sealed before a deterministic treatment-blinded sample is inspected for generality, proof collapse, local elegance, family elegance, corpus compression, and plausible human value. Critique cannot change the mechanical result, and any protocol deviation forces a Null Result. Scientific Confirmation or Null Result both transition to Wrela, which is not a rescue analysis.
