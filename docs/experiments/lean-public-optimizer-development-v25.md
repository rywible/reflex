## Economics

Full used 54.592 seconds of end-to-end CPU for eight discoveries, or 6.824 seconds each. Bootstrap used 55.928 seconds for four, or 13.982 seconds each. Full was therefore about 2.0× more CPU-efficient per verified discovery end to end while finding twice as many results.

The fresh-Candidate economics are stronger. Full found all eight after 28 Candidate checks and 1.762 seconds of Candidate Verification CPU, about 0.220 seconds per discovery. Bootstrap found four after 120 checks and 10.064 seconds, about 2.516 seconds each. Those final points are not equal-prefix comparisons, so the frozen request-16 result remains the primary causal ranking evidence.

Full and no-model import retained training state. Mandatory replay consumes 100 of their 128 total Verification requests, leaving 28 fresh Candidate checks. Bootstrap starts Fresh, spends eight Seed replays, and has 120 fresh checks. This is intentional total-envelope economics: retained verified knowledge is not free. The request-16 curve separately compares learned and Bootstrap ranking at equal fresh-Candidate prefixes.

## Ablations

No-model found only two collapses and removed 405 nodes under the same imported state and fresh-Candidate allowance. This isolates strong causal ranking value in the promoted model.

No-derived matched Full exactly. Its seed bundle was byte-identical to the training bundle, proving that training had produced no active Derived Operators for this corpus. The experiment therefore says nothing positive or negative about Derived Operator value; that mechanism was absent rather than causally neutralized after use.

The phase-zero information audit reconstructed all 203 proof-substitution attempts without ambiguity or unmatched donors. It found no Accepted/Refuted feature-collision group. The current features were therefore sufficient to separate observed decisions on this training corpus, although that does not establish calibration on a broader population.

## Interpretation and limits

The learned CPU model now shows scaling causal value through the public Improvement Session: doubling the held-out Development claims from four to eight preserved the 4-to-1 request-16 advantage and expanded the final advantage to 8-to-4 over Bootstrap and 8-to-2 over no-model.

Every result is still exact-statement theorem reuse from the pre-2025 library. These are valuable verified proof deduplications and refactorings, not theorem invention, abstraction discovery, or a test of 2026 mathematical taste. The held-out set is opportunity-selected and has only eight claims, so this is not Scientific Confirmation or a population-level generalization estimate.

The run used release-mode AArch64 NEON, six worker threads on CPUs 0–6, a 40 GiB supervisor boundary, one reserved CPU, and 16 GiB reserved memory. Peak treatment residency was 23.004 GB; the host reserve remained intact.

## Decision

The linear model decisively clears activation and CPU-economics gates at 32×8 breadth. Increasing parameter count remains unjustified: the phase-zero audit found no observed feature collision, and the current model already saturated all eight opportunities. The next high-value experiment should broaden independent claims while freezing this model and policy, then test a harder operator family where candidates are not exact-statement donor substitutions. That is the shortest path from proof retrieval toward verified proof construction without confusing capacity with data or search quality.

This is positive Development evidence, not Scientific Confirmation.
