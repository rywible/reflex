# Lean taste Development terminology erratum

The v1–v3 Development reports labeled head 5 “migration survival” and head 6 “verification cost.” Their raw values actually encode eligible declaration-name survival and later direct-dependency burden, respectively. They do **not** encode successful replay of the earlier proof body or measured kernel CPU.

The reports remain retained as the exact historical evidence, including their original field order and hashes. The implementation and all later protocols use the accurate names `declaration-survival` and `dependency-cost`. Exact Semantic Migration outcomes and measured kernel Verification work remain separate Measurements and gates. No numerical result changed as a consequence of this terminology correction.
